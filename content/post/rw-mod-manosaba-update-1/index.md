### 更新内容

**版本:** v1.0.1 $\to$ v1.0.2

- Now you can choose whether or not adding the narehate mutant into the "Assign" tab.
  You can change this in "Witch's Factor Settings - Misc - Allow Assign Tab for Narehate Mutants".
  Changing this option requires restarting the game to take effect.
